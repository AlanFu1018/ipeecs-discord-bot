# Registration Procedure for New Students
# 新生註冊程序

> **Note:** Most NCU services (registration, tuition, course selection, grades, etc.) are accessed through a single **Portal** login. Exact dates and deadlines change every semester — always check the official announcement from the Registry Office (註冊組) or International Affairs Office (OIA) for your semester.
>
> **注意：** 中央大學的註冊、學費、選課、成績等多數服務，皆透過單一 **Portal** 登入使用。確切日期與期限每學期不同，請務必以註冊組或國際事務處（OIA）當學期的官方公告為準。

## Step 1 — Activate Your NCU Account
## 步驟一 — 啟用中央大學帳號

Before you can log in to anything, you must activate your NCU account.

登入任何系統之前，必須先啟用中央大學帳號。

Go to: **<https://ncu.edu.tw/q/freshman-account-activation>**
(direct link: <https://tarot.cc.ncu.edu.tw/UnixAccount/enableaccount.php>)

前往：**<https://ncu.edu.tw/q/freshman-account-activation>**
（直接連結：<https://tarot.cc.ncu.edu.tw/UnixAccount/enableaccount.php>）

1. Enter your Student ID.
   輸入學號。
2. Enter your ID number (international students: enter your Student ID again in this field).
   輸入身分證字號（國際學生：此欄再次輸入學號）。
3. Enter your birth date (Western/AD calendar).
   輸入出生日期（西元）。
4. Set your own password.
   自行設定密碼。
   - 8–20 characters
     8–20 個字元
   - Must mix letters and numbers; the first character must be a letter
     須混合英文字母與數字，且第一個字元必須是英文字母
   - Cannot be similar to your Student ID or ID number
     不可與學號或身分證字號相似
5. Submit. Your account becomes active about **10 minutes** later.
   送出。帳號約 **10 分鐘**後生效。

This one account is used for: NCU Mail, Portal login, Wi-Fi authentication, Computer Center terminal login, and dorm network registration.

此帳號可用於：中央大學信箱、Portal 登入、Wi-Fi 認證、電算中心終端機登入，以及宿舍網路註冊。

## Step 2 — Log In to the NCU Portal
## 步驟二 — 登入中央大學 Portal

Go to: **<https://portal.ncu.edu.tw>**

前往：**<https://portal.ncu.edu.tw>**

- Log in with your Student ID and the password you just set.
  以學號及剛設定的密碼登入。
- Click "Settings" in the top-right corner to switch the interface to English if needed.
  如有需要，可點選右上角「Settings」將介面切換為英文。
- The Portal is the single sign-on (SSO) gateway for nearly all NCU systems — you generally don't need to log in separately to each one.
  Portal 是中央大學幾乎所有系統的單一登入（SSO）入口，一般不需要分別登入各系統。

## Step 3 — Confirm Your Personal Registration Data
## 步驟三 — 確認個人學籍資料

Every student must confirm/update their personal registration data every semester, in the Portal:

每位學生每學期都必須在 Portal 確認／更新個人學籍資料：

**NCU Portal → 便捷窗口 (Quick Window) → 服務櫃台 (iNCU) → 教務專區 (Academic Affairs) → 學籍/註冊 (Student Status / Registration) → 學籍登入 (Register Personal Data)**

Double-check especially:

請特別確認：

- Your **English name** — must be complete and exactly match your passport
  **英文姓名** — 須完整且與護照完全相同
- Your **contact/permanent address**
  **聯絡／永久地址**
- Other personal details requested on the form
  表單上要求的其他個人資料

## Step 4 — Get and Print Your Tuition Payment Slip
## 步驟四 — 取得並列印學雜費繳費單

**Option A — Through the Portal:**

**方式 A — 透過 Portal：**

NCU Portal → Student Services (學生服務) → 生活助學服務 → 學費繳費單 (Tuition Payment Slip)

**Option B — Directly through First Bank's e-School portal:**

**方式 B — 直接透過第一銀行 e-School 網站：**

<https://eschool.firstbank.com.tw>

1. Choose school type ("大專校院")
   選擇學校類型（「大專校院」）
2. Choose school name: 國立中央大學 (NCU)
   選擇學校名稱：國立中央大學
3. Enter your Student ID
   輸入學號
4. Enter the last 6 digits of your ID/ARC number
   輸入身分證／居留證號碼後 6 碼
5. Click "確定" to view and print your slip
   點選「確定」以檢視並列印繳費單

## Step 5 — Pay the Tuition
## 步驟五 — 繳交學雜費

Pick whichever is most convenient:

選擇最方便的方式：

| Method 方式 | How 說明 |
|---|---|
| **In person 臨櫃** | Pay at any First Bank branch. First Bank's Zhongli branch also visits campus every **Tue / Thu / Fri, 10:00–12:00**, at the Administrative Building, 1F.<br>至第一銀行任一分行繳費。第一銀行中壢分行每週 **二、四、五 10:00–12:00** 也會到校，地點在行政大樓一樓。 |
| **ATM transfer ATM 轉帳** | Insert your chip debit card → enter PIN → select "繳費 (Payment)" → bank code **007** → enter the 16-digit account number printed on your slip → enter the amount → confirm.<br>插入晶片金融卡 → 輸入密碼 → 選擇「繳費」→ 銀行代碼 **007** → 輸入繳費單上的 16 位數帳號 → 輸入金額 → 確認。 |
| **Credit card 信用卡** | Via the First Bank e-School portal (link above), or by phone at (02) 2760-8818 (NCU school code: **8814600440**).<br>透過第一銀行 e-School 網站（連結如上），或電話 (02) 2760-8818 繳費（中央大學學校代碼：**8814600440**）。 |
| **Convenience store 便利商店** | Pay with the printed slip at 7-Eleven, FamilyMart, etc. A NT$10 service fee applies.<br>持列印的繳費單至 7-Eleven、全家等便利商店繳費，需支付手續費 NT$10。 |
| **Taiwan Pay 台灣 Pay** | Scan the QR code on your slip using the "第e行動" app.<br>以「第e行動」App 掃描繳費單上的 QR code。 |

**When you can print a payment receipt afterward:**

**繳費後可列印收據的時間：**

- ATM / online transfer: next day
  ATM／網路轉帳：隔天
- Credit card: 3–4 working days
  信用卡：3–4 個工作天
- Convenience store: 5–7 working days
  便利商店：5–7 個工作天

*(Banks are closed during the Chinese New Year holiday period — plan ahead if your registration period overlaps with it.)*

*（農曆春節期間銀行不營業，若註冊期間與春節重疊，請提早安排。）*

## Step 6 — Get Your Student Card
## 步驟六 — 領取學生證

- Collect your student card from your department office once your registration is complete.
  完成註冊後，至系辦領取學生證。
