*Frequently Asked Questions (Bilingual: Chinese / English)*

# Registration　報到註冊

**Q: What is the procedures of registration?**

**問：報到註冊的程序為何？**

**A: Please apply through the foreign degree student application system according to the announcement time from the International Office.**

**答：請依照國際處公告時間，至外國學位生專用申請系統申請**

**Q: When should I select the courses?**

**問：何時應選課？**

**A:** Check website [http://pdc.adm.ncu.edu.tw/eng/en_index.asp](https://cis.ncu.edu.tw/Course/main/news/announce)

**答：**請查詢網站 [http://pdc.adm.ncu.edu.tw/eng/en_index.asp](https://cis.ncu.edu.tw/Course/main/news/announce)

**Q: What do I need for registration?**

**問：報到需要準備哪些文件？**

**A:** For new student, you need all of document that OIA gave to you, healthy check examination, passport, diploma and transcript certificate.

**答：**新生須準備國際事務處（OIA）提供的所有文件、健康檢查證明、護照、畢業證書及成績單。

**Q: Where can I find the information about my class schedule and location of my class?**

**問：在哪裡可以查詢課表及上課地點？**

**A:** Check website [中央大學入口網站](https://portal.ncu.edu.tw/)

**答：**請查詢網站 [中央大學入口網站](https://portal.ncu.edu.tw/) ，並點選 Services for Academic Affairs -\> Course Selection System

**Q: If I have problem about my registration, where can I have an assistant?**

**問：若報到遇到問題，可以向誰求助？**

**A:** You can have an assistant from Office of International Affair.

**答：**可向國際事務處（OIA）尋求協助。

**Q: If I late for registration, what should I do?**

**問：若報到遲到該怎麼辦？**

**A:** It will be difficult if you late for the registration, because the Office of International Affair will not take any responsibility for your registration and you have to register by yourself. You still can ask Office of International Affair for help, they will tell you what you can do for your late registration.

**答：**遲到報到會比較麻煩，因為國際事務處將不會對您的報到負責，您必須自行辦理報到手續。不過仍可向國際事務處詢問可行的處理方式。

**Q: Can I extend the term of paying the tuition fee?**

**問：可以延長繳交學費的期限嗎？**

**A:** Yes, you can extend the payment for a week after the deadline. You should go to the First Bank on the first floor of Administration Building for the payment.

Note: Service Hour in Tuesday, Thursday and Friday at 10.30am – 11.30 am. (Tel: 57392)

**答：**可以，可延後一週繳費。請至行政大樓一樓第一銀行辦理繳費。

　　備註：服務時間為週二、週四、週五上午10:30–11:30。（分機：57392）

**Q: Can I pay my tuition fee in installments?**

**問：可以分期繳納學費嗎？**

**A:** No, you have to pay all of your tuition fee at once.

**答：**不行，必須一次繳清全部學費。

**Q: How do I pay the tuition fee?**

**問：如何繳交學費？**

**A:** There are five payment methods:  
(1) In-person payment at any branch of First Bank nationwide (First Bank provides on-campus service from 10:30 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. every Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday on the 1st floor of the Administration Building.)  
(2) Payment by ATM transfer  
(3) Payment by credit card via telephone voice service  
(4) Online credit card payment  
(5) Payment at convenience stores: A NT\$10 service fee must be paid by the payer.

**答：**繳費方式有五：

\(1\) 臨櫃繳款-第一銀行全省分行 (第一銀行於每週二、四、五上午10:30-12:00會配合到校服務/行政大樓一樓)

\(2\) ATM轉帳繳費

\(3\) 語音信用卡繳費

\(4\) 網路信用卡繳費

\(5\) 超商繳款：須自付手續費10元。

# Course Selection　選課

**問：如何辦理選課登記？**

**A:** (1) Check website https://portal.ncu.edu.tw

\(2\) Choose course selection system.

\(i\) You can use "Search the Course" to find courses.

\(ii\) Use "Add and Drop Courses" to apply courses.

**答：**(1) 查詢網站 https://portal.ncu.edu.tw

　　(2) 選擇「選課系統」。

　　(i) 使用「課程查詢」搜尋課程。

　　(ii) 使用「加退選」申請課程。

**問：已選的課程可以更改嗎？**

**答：**可以，可在加退選期間更改已選課程，加退選期間將公告於 https://portal.ncu.edu.tw。

**Q: If I want to take a required course but there is no seat left, what should I do?**

**問：想修必修課但名額已滿該怎麼辦？**

**A:** If that course is a requirement course, you can ask the professor of the course for the password of course selection.

**答：**若該課程為必修課，可向授課教授索取選課密碼。

**Q: How many credits do I need to take in order to graduate?**

**問：畢業需修習多少學分？**

**A:** You have to take 128 credits from the department (or associated department).

**答：**須修滿本系（或相關系所）128學分。

**Q: Can I put my name in waiting list for courses?**

**問：可以將姓名列入候補名單嗎？**

**A:** If you really want to take the course, you can discuss with the professor of the course.

**答：**若真的想修該課程，可與授課教授討論。

**Q: What should I do if I have crushed schedule of require course?**

**問：必修課時間衝堂該怎麼辦？**

**A:** You have to give up one of them, or the system will drop the courses which crushed automatically.

**答：**必須放棄其中一門課，否則系統將自動刪除衝堂的課程。

**Q: Do we have comprehensive exam for graduation?**

**問：畢業是否需要綜合考試？**

**A:** No, there is no comprehensive exam to graduation. For master student, you have to present the result of research (oral defense). For Ph.D. students, you have to attend seminar every semester before passing the qualify test.

Ph.D. students should pass the qualify test before the 6th semester coming, otherwise, doctoral degree will be rejected. The publication requirement of Ph.D. graduation is more than 2 "EI, SCI or SSCI" papers.

**答：**不需要綜合考試。碩士生須進行研究成果口試（口試答辯）。博士生於通過資格考前，須每學期參加專題討論（seminar）。

　　博士生應於第6學期開始前通過資格考，否則將無法取得博士學位；博士畢業另須發表2篇以上EI、SCI或SSCI期刊論文。

**Q: What is the passing score to pass every courses?**

**問：每門課程及格分數為何？**

**A:** You have to reach 60 to pass the course.

**答：**須達60分方為及格。

**Q: If I retake the course, what will happen to my previous credit?**

**問：重修課程對之前的學分有何影響？**

**A:** The credit of retake course will not include in the graduation credit.

**答：**重修課程之學分不計入畢業學分。

**Q: Will my previous grade record if I retake the course?**

**問：重修課程後，先前的成績是否仍會記錄？**

**A:** Both of grades will recode in your transcript.

**答：**是，兩次成績皆會記錄於成績單上。

**Q: Where can I find the transferring credit from previous university?**

**問：如何辦理先前就讀學校的學分抵免？**

**A:** You can hand in the transcripts to teacher assistant office and tell them which course you want to transfer. They will help you to make sure which course you can transfer.

**答：**可將成績單交至助教辦公室，並告知欲抵免之課程，助教將協助確認可抵免的課程。

**Q: What document do I need to get credit transferred?**

**問：辦理學分抵免需要哪些文件？**

**A:** Hand in transcripts to teacher assistant office.

\(1\) Check website https://portal.ncu.edu.tw/ (choose English version)

\(2\) Select form

\(3\) Choose application form for the waiver and transfer of the courses then download it. (Fill the form and print)

**答：**須將成績單交至助教辦公室。

　　(1) 查詢網站 https://portal.ncu.edu.tw/（選擇英文版）

　　(2) 選擇表單

　　(3) 選擇「學分抵免申請表」並下載（填寫後列印）

**Q: If I late to select the course, what should I do?**

**問：選課遲到該怎麼辦？**

**A:** You can do add and drop manual

• Check http://course.ncu.edu.tw/Course/main/login

• Key in add/drop application form → instructor's signature → cashier office → curriculum office. (Administration Building 3rd F)

• Handling fee

**答：**可辦理人工加退選：

　　• 查詢 http://course.ncu.edu.tw/Course/main/login

　　• 需繳交手續費

# Dormitory　宿舍

**Q: Can we live off campus?**

**問：可以校外賃居嗎？**

**A:** Yes, you can live off campus if you want.

**答：**可以，若您願意可在校外居住。

**Q: Where can I registry our dormitory?**

**問：哪裡可以辦理宿舍登記？**

**A:** You can go to "Office of Student Dormitory Service" in front of female dormitory (G14).

**答：**可至「學生宿舍服務組」辦理，位於女生宿舍（G14）對面。

**Q: When the dormitory registration open every semester?**

**問：每學期宿舍登記何時開放？**

**A:** You can check in website. <https://shsd.ncu.edu.tw/>

**答：**請查詢網站：<https://shsd.ncu.edu.tw/>

**Q: How many people in one room?**

**問：一間房間住幾人？**

**A:** There are 2 to 4 people in one room.

**答：**2到4人房。

**Q: Can I choose the room?**

**問：可以自行選擇房間嗎？**

**A:** You can't choose your room by yourself, its random choosing by dormitory staff office.

**答：**不行，房間由宿舍辦公室隨機分配。

**Q: Can I change the room?**

**問：可以更換房間嗎？**

**A:** You can go to dormitory office ask for changing the room with some special reason.

**答：**若有特殊原因，可至宿舍辦公室申請更換房間。

**Q: How much we have to pay for dormitory deposit?**

**問：宿舍押金需繳多少？**

**A:** You have to pay 1000 NT for deposit, and that deposit will return when you graduate or move out from dormitory.

**答：**須繳交新台幣1000元押金，於畢業或搬離宿舍時退還。

**Q: The payment include electricity and water fee?**

**問：宿舍費是否包含水電費？**

**A:** For male and female dormitory (BM and G14) the payment include electricity and water fee but exclude air conditioner.

For International dormitory, the payment excludes electricity.

**答：**男、女生宿舍（BM、G14）含水電費，但不含冷氣費。

　　國際學生宿舍則不含電費。

**Q: The dormitory closed in summer vacation or winter vacation?**

**問：宿舍寒暑假是否關閉？**

**A:** The dormitory will not close during summer or winter vacation.

**答：**宿舍於寒暑假期間不會關閉。

**Q: Is there any explanation about dormitory regulation?**

**問：是否有宿舍規範說明？**

**A:** File "The Regulation of Student Dormitories in National Central University".

**答：**請參閱「國立中央大學學生宿舍規則」文件。

**Q: Do we have to register for dormitory every semester?**

**問：每學期都需要重新登記宿舍嗎？**

**A:** No, you don't need. Just register once.

**答：**不需要，只需登記一次即可。

**Q: If we have problem about dormitory, where we should ask?**

**問：宿舍若有問題可向誰詢問？**

**A:** There is an administrator in dormitory office.

**答：**宿舍辦公室設有專責管理人員可供詢問。

**Q: How much we should pay for dormitory every semester?**

**問：**You can check in website. [**https://shsd.ncu.edu.tw/Dormitory/ElecPrice**](https://shsd.ncu.edu.tw/Dormitory/ElecPrice)

**答：詳情請參閱網站<https://shsd.ncu.edu.tw/Dormitory/ElecPrice>**

**Q: Can I change our dormitory?**

**問：可以更換宿舍棟別嗎？**

**A:** Yes, you can change dormitory by apply.

**答：**可以，須提出申請。

**Q: What kind of facility that we can get in dormitory?**

**問：宿舍內有哪些設施？**

**A:** There are kitchen and washing machine in dormitory.

**答：**宿舍內設有廚房及洗衣機。

**Q: Can I bring kitchen supplies to the room?**

**問：可以攜帶廚具到房間嗎？**

**A:** For G14 and BM, you can't bring that. But you can bring to the room in international dormitory.

**答：**女、男生宿舍（G14、BM）不可攜帶廚具進房；國際學生宿舍則可以。

**Q: What is the procedure of live off campus?**

**問：搬出校外居住的手續為何？**

**A:** You can go to the dormitory office and hand in the check-out form. You can download the form on web or get the form from the dormitory office.

**答：**須至宿舍辦公室繳交退宿申請表，表格可於網路下載或至辦公室索取。

**Q: Can I change the property of my room in dormitory?**

**問：可以自行變更房間內部設施嗎？**

**A:** No, you can't.

**答：**不行。

**Q: Can my friends who doesn't live in dormitory stay in my room?**

**問：未住宿的朋友可以留宿於我的房間嗎？**

**A:** That is not allowable.

**答：**不允許。

**Q: Is there a "night hour" in the dormitory?**

**問：宿舍是否有門禁時間？**

**A:** There is no "night hour" in the dormitory.

**答：**宿舍沒有門禁時間。

**Q: Should I check in dormitory every semester?**

**問：每學期都需要辦理入住登記嗎？**

**A:** You don't need to check in dormitory every semester. For foreign student, they will be automatically registered in each semester unless you want to move to another place.

**答：**不需要。外籍學生除非欲搬離，否則每學期將自動續住登記。
