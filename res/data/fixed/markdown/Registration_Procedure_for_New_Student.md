# Registration Procedure for New Students

> **Note:** NCU has modernized its systems since the original 2015 guide. Most services (registration, tuition, course selection, grades, etc.) are now unified under a single **Portal** login instead of separate systems. Exact dates and deadlines change every semester — always check the official announcement from the Registry Office (註冊組) or International Affairs Office (OIA) for your semester. 

## Step 1 — Activate Your NCU Account

Before you can log in to anything, you must activate your NCU account (used to be called the "SPARC" account).

Go to: **<https://ncu.edu.tw/q/freshman-account-activation>**
(direct link: <https://tarot.cc.ncu.edu.tw/UnixAccount/enableaccount.php>)

1. Enter your Student ID.
2. Enter your ID number (international students: enter your Student ID again in this field).
3. Enter your birth date (Western/AD calendar).
4. Set your own password.
   - 8–20 characters
   - Must mix letters and numbers; the first character must be a letter
   - Cannot be similar to your Student ID or ID number
5. Submit. Your account becomes active about **10 minutes** later.

This one account is used for: NCU Mail, Portal login, Wi-Fi authentication, Computer Center terminal login, and dorm network registration.

## Step 2 — Log In to the NCU Portal

Go to: **<https://portal.ncu.edu.tw>**

- Log in with your Student ID and the password you just set.
- Click "Settings" in the top-right corner to switch the interface to English if needed.
- The Portal is now the single sign-on (SSO) gateway for nearly all NCU systems — you generally don't need to log in separately to each one.

## Step 3 — Confirm Your Personal Registration Data

Every student must confirm/update their personal registration data every semester. This used to be a separate "教務系統" website; it is now inside the Portal:

**NCU Portal → 便捷窗口 (Quick Window) → 服務櫃台 (iNCU) → 教務專區 (Academic Affairs) → 學籍/註冊 (Student Status / Registration) → 學籍登入 (Register Personal Data)**

Double-check especially:

- Your **English name** — must be complete and exactly match your passport
- Your **contact/permanent address**
- Other personal details requested on the form

## Step 4 — Get and Print Your Tuition Payment Slip

**Option A — Through the Portal:**

NCU Portal → Student Services (學生服務) → 生活助學服務 → 學費繳費單 (Tuition Payment Slip)

**Option B — Directly through First Bank's e-School portal:**

<https://eschool.firstbank.com.tw>

1. Choose school type ("大專校院")
2. Choose school name: 國立中央大學 (NCU)
3. Enter your Student ID
4. Enter the last 6 digits of your ID/ARC number
5. Click "確定" to view and print your slip

## Step 5 — Pay the Tuition

Pick whichever is most convenient:

| Method | How |
|---|---|
| **In person** | Pay at any First Bank branch. First Bank's Zhongli branch also visits campus every **Tue / Thu / Fri, 10:00–12:00**, at the Administrative Building, 1F. |
| **ATM transfer** | Insert your chip debit card → enter PIN → select "繳費 (Payment)" → bank code **007** → enter the 16-digit account number printed on your slip → enter the amount → confirm. |
| **Credit card** | Via the First Bank e-School portal (link above), or by phone at (02) 2760-8818 (NCU school code: **8814600440**). |
| **Convenience store** | Pay with the printed slip at 7-Eleven, FamilyMart, etc. A NT$10 service fee applies. |
| **Taiwan Pay** | Scan the QR code on your slip using the "第e行動" app. |

**When you can print a payment receipt afterward:**
- ATM / online transfer: next day
- Credit card: 3–4 working days
- Convenience store: 5–7 working days

*(Banks are closed during the Chinese New Year holiday period — plan ahead if your registration period overlaps with it.)*

## Step 6 — Get Your Student Card

- Collect your student card from your department office once your registration is complete (new students only need to do this once; returning students may need their card stamped each semester instead — check with the Registry Office for the current process).
