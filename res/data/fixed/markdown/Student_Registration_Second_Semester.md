# Student Registration — Second Semester

> ⚠️ **Updated version.** Exact class-start and payment dates change every semester — always check the Registry Office's (註冊組) official announcement for the current semester's dates. The steps below have been updated to match NCU's current systems (Portal-based registration, and the fully online Work Permit application).

## Registration

### Registration Procedure

**I. Download the tuition pay-in slip** *(check the Registry Office announcement for this semester's exact payment period)*

> Note: The pay-in slip includes the tuition, dormitory, insurance and other fees.

**A. Login Portal with your student ID No. and password**

1. Go to [www.ncu.edu.tw](http://www.ncu.edu.tw) => Click "Portal 入口", or go directly to <https://portal.ncu.edu.tw>
2. Login with your student ID No. and password.
3. Click "Tuition Fee" (or: Portal → Student Services (學生服務) → 生活助學服務 → 學費繳費單)
4. Print the slip.

**B. First Bank** — <https://eschool.firstbank.com.tw/member/index.aspx>

> Choose NCU「國立中央大學」, then fill in your student ID No. and the last 6 digits of your ID/ARC number.

**II. Pay the tuition** in the First Bank or convenience store with the printed payment slip.

- **Service Hour at NCU Administrative Building 1F:** Tuesday, Thursday, Friday 10:30~11:30
- **Deadline:** check the Registry Office's announcement for this semester (payment via First Bank vs. convenience store usually have different cutoff dates)
- Payment options: in person at First Bank, ATM transfer (bank code 007), credit card (First Bank e-School portal or phone (02) 2760-8818), convenience store (NT$10 fee), or Taiwan Pay QR code scan via the "第e行動" app.
- *(Banks are closed during Chinese New Year — plan ahead if your registration period overlaps with it.)*

**III. Confirm Your Registration Data** *(for Registry Office)*

> Confirm your personal information, especially your English Name (complete and same as passport), and your contact address (the address of your own country home).

**NCU Portal → 便捷窗口 (Quick Window) → 服務櫃台 (iNCU) → 教務專區 (Academic Affairs) → 學籍/註冊 (Student Status / Registration) → 學籍登入 (Register Personal Data)**

> (This used to be a separate "教務系統" website; it is now inside the Portal. It is required to confirm personal data for every student, every semester.)

**IV. International Affairs Office**

- **A. Time:** check the current semester's OIA announcement for the date/time window.
- **B. Please submit a copy of the following documents** *(confirm current requirements with OIA — some schools have moved this online, so double-check whether in-person submission is still required):*
  1. Payment receipt
  2. ARC
  3. New students who enrolled this academic year: submit a copy of each page of the exit and reentry record in your passport.

  > Note: Please make the copy yourself at a convenience store first.

- **C. Check the personal data** — double-check the following information:
  1. Student ID No.
  2. Country
  3. Chinese Name
  4. English Name
  5. Gender
  6. Birth Date (dd/mm/yy)
  7. Grade / Level
  8. Department
  9. Dorm Room No. / Address Off Campus
  10. Passport No.
  11. ARC No.
  12. ARC Expiration
  13. Post Office Account No.
  14. Cellphone
  15. LAB Phone No.
  16. Email
  17. Advisor's Name
  18. Phone No. at Countrytown
  19. Address at Countrytown
  20. Contact Person at Countrytown / Relationship

  > Remark: Please make sure that you know the information above very well before coming to the IA office.

- **D.** Confirm that you have finished confirming your registration data online, as mentioned above in Part III.
- **E.** If you need a Work Permit this semester, see the updated process below — it is now a fully online application, so there is no paper form to collect from OIA.

NOTICE: You may need to check for your work permit to see if it is due or not.

*Guide updated based on NCU's official Portal, First Bank e-School, and Work Permit (EZ Work Permit) pages as of September 2026. Always confirm exact dates and any still-unverified steps (particularly Part IV's in-person document submission) against the current semester's official Registry Office / OIA announcement.*
